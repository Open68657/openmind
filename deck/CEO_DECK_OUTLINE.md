## Speaker notes

**1.** V already runs inside more than 400 TV brands, yet almost nobody outside the industry knows our name. In five minutes I'll show how social turns that invisible scale into market authority and consumer love.
*Q:* Why should a B2B operating system invest in social at all? *A:* Because OEMs, advertisers and buyers all check us there first, and today they find almost nothing.

**2.** Samsung sells chips and picture modes and the OS disappears; Amazon Fire TV gets closer by riding real viewing moments and quick hacks; Roku owns culture with Roku City. The further a brand moves from specs to culture, the bigger its audience: Roku has 3.4 times Tizen's LinkedIn following, and that's the direction we take.
*Q:* Isn't Roku a consumer device brand, unlike us? *A:* So is every TV we power; we win the living room the same way.

**3.** Two engines with two clear jobs: LinkedIn, already at 13K followers, builds authority with OEMs, partners and advertisers, while Instagram, dormant since March 2025, relaunches in December to win the people on the sofa. A YouTube masterclass library and short-form video hubs, shot on our own in-house production days, feed both engines.
*Q:* Why not TikTok and everything else too? *A:* Focus: two engines done brilliantly beat six done averagely.

**4.** LinkedIn is where we sell what only V has: insight from more than 400 TV brands, partnerships like OpenAI, Teads and Hisense, and above all your own voice, backed by Denis. Your post on getting close to number one with Hisense and 400 brands is exactly the tone that makes OEMs and media buyers call us first.
*Q:* Do I have to post personally? *A:* Twice a month, fully drafted for you; ten minutes, and it's our strongest B2B reach.

**5.** We relaunch the dormant account with one living room spread across nine tiles, Where Moments Come Alive, so the launch itself is the statement. Then we stay always-on with real living-room moments, quick feature tips and the smart home, so the product is always the hero of the feed.
*Q:* Will a dormant account really come back? *A:* The 9-grid is the reset button; the formats are built to earn the follow.

**6.** This is where the product becomes the star: 15-second videos that show how fast V is, how it connects the smart home, and the one shortcut that saves you time tonight. Every clip sells a real capability, from finding content instantly to low-latency gaming and sports picture modes, in the time it takes to scroll.
*Q:* Why would anyone watch a video about an operating system? *A:* Because it's 15 seconds and solves a real problem on their couch.

**7.** Every industry event gets the same four-week playbook: tease it, go live, bring it back home to the living room, then return to routine, with Instagram and LinkedIn each playing their own role. CES in January is the template, and we repeat it for ASEAN, CTV Summit London, StreamTV, IFA, DisruptTV and Black Friday.
*Q:* Isn't this a lot of content around one trade show? *A:* One shoot at the booth feeds a full month on both channels.

**8.** The engine behind all of this is simple: a monthly content calendar planned week by week, batch production days every two months, and a monthly performance review that sharpens what comes next. All I need today is your green light for the December relaunch and the CES activation.
*Q:* What will I see before CES to know it's working? *A:* A monthly performance review, starting with the first month.
