# V on Social: CEO Deck (7 slides / 5 minutes)

Visual system: background #0A0A0A, cards #171717, white #FFFFFF, secondary grey #9A9A9A, V gold #F5C518. Font: Montserrat (Bold for titles, Regular for subtitles). One headline, one subtitle, visuals, and at most 3 gold pill badges per slide. The voiceover and Q&A are also in each slide's speaker notes.

## Slide 3: The Strategy
- **Title:** Two Channels. Two Jobs. One Brand.
- **Subtitle:** LinkedIn wins the deal. Instagram wins the living room.
- **Layout:** A 50/50 split. Left: a black card "B2B / LINKEDIN: Win OEMs & Advertisers" with a LinkedIn mockup. Right: a white card "B2C / INSTAGRAM: Win the Living Room" with an Instagram mockup. The contrast itself makes the point.
- **Assets:** `03_linkedin_preview.png` (a LinkedIn feed frame with an exec post from the Plan PDF), `03_instagram_preview.png` (a small crop of the 9-grid).
- **Badges:** B2B: pipeline · B2C: preference · One visual language
- **Voiceover:** We deliberately split the work: LinkedIn is our sales tool for OEMs and advertisers, and Instagram is our brand tool for the people on the sofa. Same black, white and gold identity, but each channel has one business job and one scorecard.
- **CEO Q:** Why not just be everywhere? **A:** Focus wins: two channels done brilliantly beat seven done averagely.

- **Subtitle:** Data and leadership voices that make OEMs and advertisers call us first.
- **Layout:** One large card on the left, two portrait cards on the right.
- **Assets (Plan PDF):** `04_mckinsey_viewing_graph.png` (the McKinsey viewing data graph as a branded data post), `04_exec_post_1.png` (executive post mockup), `04_exec_post_2.png` (second executive post or the OEM partnership announcement card).

- **Subtitle:** Real homes, real moments. V is the quiet hero of the living room.
- **Layout:** A phone frame with a gold outline on the left, plus 3 tall lifestyle images.

## Slide 6: Amplifiers (YouTube + Creators)
- **Title:** Creators Make V Famous Faster.
- **Subtitle:** YouTube and ambassadors multiply reach we don't have to buy.
- **Layout:** A wide YouTube card on the left, plus 3 narrow creator cards.
- **Assets (Plan PDF):** `06_youtube_thumbnails.png` (the YouTube channel / thumbnail mockups), `06_creator_1/2/3.png` (the creator / ambassador cards).
- **Badges:** Borrowed trust · Always-on video · Lower cost per reach
- **Voiceover:** YouTube gives V a permanent home for demos and stories, and creators and ambassadors carry our message into audiences that would never follow a tech brand. It is the fastest way to earn trust, because people believe people, not logos.
- **CEO Q:** How do we control what creators say? **A:** Clear brief, approval gate, paid only on delivery.

## Slide 7: Decision
- **Title:** Greenlight: 90 Days to Launch V.
- **Subtitle:** Three phases. Every phase tied to a business number.
- **Layout:** A 3-column timeline with a gold line at the top (Ignite / Prove / Scale), plus a full-width gold bar with the ask.
- **Ask bar:** THE ASK: approve budget [ $X ] + one CEO post per month
- **Badges:** KPI: OEM & advertiser leads · KPI: brand search & followers · 90-day review
- **Voiceover:** I'm asking for one decision today: approve the 90-day plan and the budget, and lend us your voice once a month on LinkedIn. In 90 days I'll come back with the numbers that matter: OEM and advertiser leads, and how many more people search for V by name.
- **CEO Q:** What if it doesn't work? **A:** Day-60 checkpoint: we cut what doesn't convert and move the money to what does.
